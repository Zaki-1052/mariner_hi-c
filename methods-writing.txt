## AI failure modes to avoid                                                    
                                                                                
These are drawn from a prior writing session on the same project (grant         
statistical methodology). Each failure appeared during actual writing and had   
to be corrected.                                                                
                                                                                
**Dramatizing standard methods.** TMM normalization is standard. edgeR is an    
established tool. Gene-body aggregation is what people do for mCH. VC           
normalization was the method. Do not over-justify established practices or      
narrate them as discoveries. State what was done.                               
                                                                                
**Substituting numbers for plain facts.** AI has a tendency to throw in         
specific numbers ("3x more sites," "10,000 bp tolerance") to make standard      
choices sound like benchmarking results. A methods section states the method    
and the parameter. Results belong in results.                                   
                                                                                
**Fix narratives.** VC normalization is the method. Do not frame it as a        
fallback for unavailable KR, a workaround, or a correction. The 250402 KR       
balancing fix is pipeline maintenance, not methods content. State the           
normalization used.                                                             
                                                                                
**Negative parallelisms.** "This is not X, it is Y" and all variants. The       
single most reliable AI tell. Just state Y.                                     
                                                                                
**Shadowboxing against strawmen.** "Specified in advance rather than selected   
post hoc" is AI defending against imagined bad science. Nobody needs to be told 
you are not doing bad science. Detected and corrected in the prior session.     
                                                                                
**"Framework" as AI-ism.** Use "pipeline" or the tool name. "edgeR              
quasi-likelihood F-test" is the test. Not the "edgeR framework" or the          
"quasi-likelihood framework."                                                   
                                                                                
**Pontificating about pipeline history.** Do not spend time on how analytical   
decisions were arrived at, what was tried first, what legacy configs exist, or  
how the pipeline evolved from n=1 to n=3 replicates. State the method as it is. 
                                                                                
**Hedging.** A methods section states what was done. No "may," "could,"         
"might."                                                                        
                                                                                
**Over-justifying standard choices.** "The robust flag protects against outlier 
genes" is a textbook explanation, not methods content. State that `robust =     
TRUE` was used. The reviewer knows what it does.                                
                                                                                
**Slop vocabulary contamination.** Your language shapes Zara's mental model of  
how to write the methods. Clean language is a requirement. Every word on the    
banned list will contaminate her thinking about how to phrase the methods       
section.

⏺ Section 4 in plain language:

  HiCCUPS calls loops at three resolutions independently. A physical loop in the
  genome might show up in the 5 kb calls, the 10 kb calls, and the 25 kb calls,
  but at slightly different coordinates because the genomic bins are different
  sizes. The multi-resolution comparison asks: are any of these the "same" loop?

  The matching rule: take a loop from the 5 kb list. Look at the 10 kb list.
  Find any loop on the same chromosome pair where BOTH anchors land within 10 kb
  of the 5 kb loop's anchors. If more than one 10 kb loop meets that criterion,
  pick the one where the total positional offset across both anchors is
  smallest. That total offset is the "summed anchor distance" (distance between
  anchor1 positions + distance between anchor2 positions).

  The sentence "The closest match was selected by summed anchor distance" is
  trying to say: the match with the smallest summed anchor distance was
  selected. "Selected by" is slightly ambiguous because "by" could modify
  "selected" (how it was selected) or "match" (which match). Rewording to "The
  match with the smallest summed anchor distance was selected" would be
  unambiguous, but it is 11 words vs 9.

  Your call on whether to reword. Now let me fix the versions table.
