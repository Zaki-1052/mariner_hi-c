  4. Mechanistic Interpretation

  ---
  5. Biological Interpretation

  Why CG methylation dominates:

  1. DNMT enzymes are CpG-specific: DNA methyltransferases (DNMT1, DNMT3A/B)
  preferentially methylate CpG dinucleotides
  2. Maintenance methylation: DNMT1 specifically recognizes hemimethylated CpG
  sites after DNA replication, ensuring inheritance
  3. Non-CpG methylation is cell-type specific: Found mainly in:
    - Embryonic stem cells
    - Neurons (especially gene bodies)
    - Oocytes
  4. 5hmC is almost exclusively at CpG: TET enzymes (which convert 5mC to 5hmC)
  act primarily at CpG sites

  Why CHG/CHH show no BAP1-related changes:

  1. Baseline is too low: With only 0.6-0.9% methylation, there's minimal
  dynamic range for differential analysis
  2. High noise-to-signal ratio: The near-zero correlations (0.02-0.03 for hmC)
  indicate the data is essentially noise
  3. Biological mechanism is CpG-specific: BAP1's effect on chromatin
  accessibility and TET enzyme activity would primarily impact CpG methylation
