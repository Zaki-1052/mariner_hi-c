## One-line takeaway

The core BAP1-KO methylation phenotype is real, robust, and generalizable: it survives a genomic null model, replicates across species, propagates into 3D loop anchors and allelic asymmetry, and — when measured on an unbiased gene set — behaves exactly as the TET-block / stoichiometric-conversion mechanism predicts.

---

## Biological story

The main figures build a single causal chain: BAP1 loss → H2AK119ub accumulation → coordinated 5mC↑ / 5hmC↓ at gene bodies → a TET-mediated demethylation block, with neuronal/synaptic genes disproportionately affected because they sit in K119ub-rich Polycomb chromatin. A reviewer's natural objection to that chain is that each link is an enrichment statistic, and enrichment statistics on genomic data can be inflated by gene-density clustering, by selection bias in how gene sets are built, or by being a one-off feature of this particular mouse cohort. Figure S1 is the answer to that objection. It assembles six independent corroborations, each from a different analytical frame, that all point back to the same phenotype.

Panel A shows the statistical foundation holds: all fifteen of the gene-level cross-tabulations the paper rests on (mC↔ATAC, mC↔expression, the four DiffBind cascade directions, the compartment-shift coupling, the loop-anchor and MeCP2 relationships) survive a chromosome-stratified label-shuffle null that destroys any gene-density artifact. Panel B shows the phenotype is not mouse-specific — the human BAP1 chr8 methylation hotspot from Field et al. 2019 replicates directionally in our cerebellum at gene bodies. Panel C extends the methylation change into 3D architecture: CTCF anchors of loops *lost* in BAP1-KO are hypermethylated at their flanking dynamic CpG regions, the methylation-side signature of the Flavahan/Bernstein IDH-glioma insulator-decay model. Panels D and E are deliberately self-disciplining: Panel D shows that CpG-island hypermethylation is *not* driven by K119ub (K119ub is depleted, not enriched, at hyper islands), which correctly pins the K119ub→methylation coupling to gene bodies and dynamic regions rather than islands; Panel E shows BAP1 loss roughly doubles allele-specific methylation, tying the bulk signal to allele-resolved events. Panel F is the methodological capstone — re-running the neuronal stoichiometry on an *unbiased* GO-derived gene set (rather than a DMR-selected one) reverses the earlier "neuronal genes gain total methylation" claim: with the unbiased set, total methylation at neuronal genes actually *decreases* slightly and rises only at the methylation-defined sets where it should (coordinated, MeCP2-Up). Together, the figure converts the main story from "we found an enrichment" into "the enrichment is genuine, conserved, architecturally consequential, and we removed our own selection bias to confirm it."

## Caveats & framing

- **"Preferentially," not "exclusively."** Neuronal genes are disproportionately, not solely, affected. The companion analyses show MeCP2 redistribution tracks methylation (Coordinated × MeCP2-Up OR = 5.16) far more than neuronal identity (Neuronal × MeCP2-Up OR = 1.73). Panel F reinforces this: the methylation *gain* lives at the coordinated/MeCP2-Up sets, while broad neuronal genes lose total methylation — neuronal specificity is about which loci are K119ub-rich substrate, not about where total methylation rises.

- **MeCP2 is CUT&RUN, never ChIP.** Panel F's "MeCP2-Up" is a gene-set label derived from MeCP2 CUT&RUN DiffBind. Use "signal" or "mark" in any prose; never call MeCP2 a ChIP target. (H2AK119ub in Panels C/D *is* ChIP/peak-derived; the script correctly describes it as "K119ub peak overlap" and makes no false CUT&RUN claim.)

- **K119ub sign reconciliation.** K119ub is the *positive* driver of hypermethylation as a **differential DiffBind fold** (genes that *gain* K119ub hypermethylate — main Figures 2/7, and Panel A test 37-07d OR 14.75). At the same time, **baseline absolute K119ub** at CpG islands is *anti*-correlated with island hypermethylation (Panel D: K119ub depleted at hyper islands, OR < 1). These are compatible, not contradictory: genes that *gain* K119ub remethylate, whereas regions already drenched in constitutive K119ub (CpG islands here) are a distinct, depleted population. Panel D is specifically the island context — it does not contradict the gene-body cascade.

- **Panel F is a correction presented as the data, not flagged as a "self-correction."** The narrow-set (+0.012) vs broad-set (−0.0022) flip is a DMR-selection artifact that the unbiased GO set resolves. The figure presents the broad-set result as the answer; the contrast is the point, not a mea culpa. The Panel F subtitle was reworded during verification from "rises only at coordinated / MeCP2-Up" to "rises at methylation-defined sets (coordinated / MeCP2-Up)" because the Neur + Coord intersection (+0.0076) also rises — "only" was imprecise. No statistic or guard changed.

- **Exploratory-strength data points.** ASM per-sample significance (Panel E) is uncorrected and aggregated across samples, so absolute counts are indicative rather than definitive; the 1.95× ratio and the qualitative doubling are the robust claims. The interval-level permutation tests (sections 34–36) never wrote their comparison TSVs (they crashed at the table-emission step after computing heatmaps); Panel A therefore uses the gene-level permutation (section 37), which ran to completion and carries the formal confirmatory weight for all 15 tests.

- **n = 4 effective replicates per condition** (8 samples, 2 ctrl + 2 mut per sex; sex covariate in run-5 but sex/genotype partially confounded at n = 2/group per sex). This applies to every panel and bounds the precision of all effect-size estimates.
