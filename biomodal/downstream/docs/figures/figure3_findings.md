## One-line takeaway

The coordinated 5mC↑/5hmC↓ phenotype is caused by an **impaired (substrate-limited) TET demethylation pathway**, NOT by H2AK119ub directly recruiting DNMT3A — and at neuronal genes the mC-for-hmC exchange is **stoichiometric (slope ≈ −1, dehydroxymethylase-like)**, distinguishing those loci from the non-stoichiometric TET-inhibition signature seen genome-wide.

---

## Biological story

Figures 1–2 establish *what* happens (a coordinated, reciprocal 5mC↑/5hmC↓ shift at gene bodies) and *where the upstream signal sits* (H2AK119ub gain, concentrated in active/euchromatic chromatin). Figure 3 answers the harder mechanistic question: by what enzymatic route does that signature arise? There are two competing models. The **DNMT3A-recruitment** model says BAP1 loss raises H2AK119ub, and the K119ub-binding UDR domain of DNMT3A is recruited to deposit de-novo 5mC at those marks (Chen et al. 2024). The **TET-impediment** model says BAP1 loss instead indirectly blocks TET-mediated active demethylation — 5mC is normally oxidized to 5hmC by TET en route to erasure, so blocking that conversion accumulates 5mC at the direct expense of 5hmC while leaving total modified cytosine flat (exactly the bulk signature in Figure 1 / Section 64: +0.32% 5mC, −0.39% 5hmC, total modC unchanged). This figure adjudicates between them and the verdict is decisive: TET impediment wins.

The evidence converges from four directions. First, **substrate availability** is the strongest single predictor — genes carrying more wildtype 5hmC (more oxidized TET substrate) show the largest 5hmC loss in BAP1-KO (baseline-5hmC AUC = 0.762, far above K119ub's 0.573). Second, a head-to-head logistic comparison shows the **TET-impediment model out-discriminates the DNMT3A-recruitment model** (AUC 0.793 vs 0.696, DeLong p = 9.43e-49), and critically H2AK119ub enters the predictive model with a *negative* sign — the opposite of what direct DNMT3A recruitment would require. Third, the impairment is **strongest exactly where TET is normally most active** (active promoters/enhancers lose the most demethylation efficiency; Polycomb-repressed loci, where TET is already quiet, show no impairment or even reverse), the geography of an enzyme being throttled rather than a methyltransferase being newly recruited. Fourth, BAP1-KO reproduces only ~3% of a published TET triple-KO's effect in a graded rather than all-or-nothing fashion — a "dimmer, not an off switch," consistent with an *indirect* TET defect (impaired access/efficiency via PRC1/H2AK119ub) rather than enzyme ablation. Layered on top, the per-gene **stoichiometry** at neuronal loci (Δ5mC vs Δ5hmC slope ≈ −1) points to direct 5hmC→5mC conversion (dehydroxymethylase-like DNMT3A activity), a mechanistically distinct route from the non-stoichiometric (slope ≠ −1) TET-inhibition-plus-de-novo pattern that dominates non-neuronal genes — refining the mechanism gene-class by gene-class.

## Caveats & framing

- **"Impeded / partial," not "ablated."** The TET defect is indirect and graded. BAP1-KO recovers only ~3% (absolute) to ~9% (baseline-normalized) of a TET triple-KO's ratio shift (Panel E), and the per-gene response is continuous, not all-or-nothing. State the mechanism as *impaired TET access/efficiency via the Polycomb/H2AK119ub axis*, a dimmer switch — not as direct TET inhibition or enzyme loss. The apparent BAP1-vs-TET-KO per-gene correlation (rho 0.22) is almost entirely a shared-WT-baseline artifact (residualized rho 0.10; relative rho ≈ 0), so the two perturbations do **not** converge gene-for-gene through a shared mechanism.

- **Stoichiometry is gene-class-specific — "neuronal ≈ −1" is the contrast, not a global claim.** The slope-≈−1 (stoichiometric, dehydroxymethylase-like) result holds for the broad GO-neuronal set (−0.995) and synapse/axon set (−1.020); genome-wide the slope is significantly steeper-than-(−1) toward net mC gain (all genes −0.959; coordinated genes −1.288). Present neuronal stoichiometry as a *distinct sub-mechanism at neuronal loci*, contrasted against the non-stoichiometric majority — not as the genome-wide pattern.

- **The neuronal slope uses the unbiased broad GO set, deliberately.** Section 78's broad GO-derived neuronal set (5,614 genes; 4,118 with all-marks data) replaces section-61's DMR-selected narrow set (1,149 genes), which was circular (genes chosen *because* they had significant DMRs). The narrow set spuriously showed total methylation *increasing* at neuronal genes (+0.012); the unbiased broad set shows it *decreasing* (−0.0022). Figure 3 uses the broad set; present it as the result, not as a "correction."

- **H2AK119ub sign reconciliation (important for the manuscript).** K119ub appears with *opposite* signs across figures and these are compatible, not contradictory:
  - As a *differential* DiffBind log2FC (Figure 2 Panel B; Sections 14/33/41), genes that *gain* K119ub hypermethylate → **positive predictor** (OR ≈ 4.71).
  - As *baseline constitutive* signal in the TET-impediment model (this figure, Panel B; Section 24, standardized β ≈ −1.05), genes with high pre-existing K119ub are already compacted/repressed and inaccessible to DNMT3A → **negative predictor** of hypermethylation.
  - The reconciling statement: *gaining* K119ub drives hypermethylation in active chromatin, whereas *constitutively high* K119ub marks already-repressed regions that are protected. The manuscript should state this explicitly to avoid an apparent figure-to-figure conflict. (This is also exactly why Panel C shows impairment at active promoters and a *reversal* at Polycomb/repressed promoters.)

- **Panel B AUCs are model-discrimination on the section-24 feature set (n = 11,937 genes, 51.9% hyper-DMR).** AUC and the linear-model R² values (Section 25: TET R² = 0.259 vs DNMT3A R² = 0.044) are not on the same scale; the low absolute R² does not undercut the consistent feature hierarchy. The TET-vs-DNMT3A conclusion is robust to dichotomization (binary DMR and continuous Δ-ratio agree), to non-promoter stratification (TET still wins, 0.790 vs 0.717), across all three 5hmC tertiles, and to 10-fold CV (near-zero optimism).

- **DeLong p = 9.43e-49 has documented provenance.** The exact value lives in `dnmt3a_exclusive_model_comparison.tsv` (`delong_p` column, shared-model comparison; the exclusive-model comparison is even smaller, 6.13e-75). The script `fmt_p()` display floor would otherwise print "p < 2.2e-16" — cite the exact TSV magnitude, not the display floor.

- **TET-KO comparison is cross-platform (Panel E).** The TET triple-KO reference (Lopez-Moyado et al., GSE166423) is BS/OxBS-seq from a different tissue/protocol, so the magnitude comparison is qualitative; the script appropriately frames it via baseline-normalized relative metrics. This panel is supporting evidence — the mechanism stands on Panels A–D without it (the master plan flags it as supplemental-eligible).

- **No MeCP2 in this figure.** Figure 3 is purely the methylation-mechanism arm (DUET 5mC/5hmC + H2AK119ub mark + chromatin state). MeCP2 (which is **CUT&RUN, not ChIP**) appears only in later figures; do not import MeCP2 framing here. All H2AK119ub/H3K27me3/H3K27ac references are ChIP-derived histone marks, correctly labeled "mark"/"signal."
