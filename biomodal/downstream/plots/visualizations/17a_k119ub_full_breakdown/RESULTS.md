## Section 17: Honest K119ub Assessment at DMR Genes
**Key numbers:**
- Most genes have NO K119ub peak: mC Up 62.2% no-peaks (4,674/7,513), hmC Down 64.6% (6,150/9,521) (source: k119ub_honest_breakdown.tsv)
- Conditional gain among peak-bearing genes: mC Up = 47.8% gained (1,358/2,839), hmC Down = 41.6%, mC Down = 16.2% (source: k119ub_honest_breakdown.tsv)
- Background gain rate (all DMR genes with peaks) = 33.6% (1,954/5,811) (source: k119ub_honest_breakdown.tsv)
- mC Up gain enrichment = +14.2 pp above background; hmC Down = +8.0 pp (source: k119ub_honest_breakdown.tsv, derived)

**What this shows:** Corrects the Section 14/16c framing. Hypermethylated DMRs are genuinely enriched for K119ub gain (+14 pp over background among peak-bearing genes), but the effect is modest and applies to a minority — most DMR genes carry no K119ub peak at all (58.2% background no-peak rate). The 68.3% "mC Up → gained" claim was inflated by excluding the no-peak majority and ignoring the ~34% background.
