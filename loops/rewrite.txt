  contacts with norm=NONE (was VC)" — this was your instruction from when the
  script was created. The reasoning is that edgeR handles normalization itself

  double-normalized: once by Juicer's visibility correction, then again by
  edgeR's TMM. NONE gives edgeR the raw observed counts so TMM is the only
  normalization applied.
